Our Approach

How We Connect Projects to Capital

A four-step process that closes gaps before they reach the funder, managing the evaluation all the way to a term sheet.

  1. 01

    Fundability Checklist

    Client completes our Augere Fundability Checklist and submits the required document pack. Augere reviews the project at this stage.

  2. 02

    Six-Pillar Review

    Augere scores the project against the six-pillar framework: deal structure, legal opinion, risk records, financial standing, collateral and documentation. Mandatory qualifications ensure any funder sees a sound deal.

  3. 03

    Structuring Call

    A structuring call with the client to refine deal positioning and confirm the document pack is complete and optimally presented.

  4. 04

    Funder Submission

    Augere submits to the funder network and manages the evaluation through to a term sheet, staying with the deal, not handing it off.
